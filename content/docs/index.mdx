Decoy is a CLI-first toolkit for masking sensitive data and generating
synthetic replacements in local files and YAML-defined pipelines. You point it
at a file or folder, it profiles the data, you review what it found, and you
run a pipeline that produces a masked or synthetic output you can inspect
before it goes anywhere else.

## What ships today

- 13 masking strategies for field-level, reviewable transforms.
- 5 synthetic generation column types, plus statistical fitting for
  whole-table generation.
- 6 transform operations for shaping columns before or after masking.
- 34 source and destination providers for local files and common formats.

Every run is explicit: pipelines are plain YAML, strategies are picked per
field, and STORM profiling only ever suggests a strategy for you to confirm,
it never classifies or applies anything on its own.
